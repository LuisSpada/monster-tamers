# Asset attributions

The pixel-art files in [`public/assets`](./public/assets) are used by the
Monster Tamers student project. Assets may have been created or generated
specifically for this project, and some may have been produced with
AI-assisted tools before being selected, cropped, or adjusted for the UI.

The asset set includes the logo, navigation icons, scene/background layers,
monster sprites, encounter art, and avatars. Any future third-party asset
must be documented here with its creator, source URL, and license before use.
Do not assume or claim that an asset is original, unchanged, or third-party
unless the repository provides evidence for that claim.
